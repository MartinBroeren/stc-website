Privacy & cookies.

Website visits

You can visit the public website without creating an account. Our hosting provider necessarily processes technical request data such as IP address, browser information and timestamps to deliver and protect the site.

Necessary storage

stc_privacy_preferences is a first-party cookie that remembers your privacy choices for six months. It does not track you across websites.

Community registration

When you apply to join the STC community, we process the details you enter in the form to review your registration, maintain our member administration and send the WhatsApp community invitation. The form clearly marks required and optional fields.

Registration data is stored in a restricted Google Sheet. The invite email is sent through Zoho CPaaS. We record when you registered, which consent text was shown and whether the invitation was sent.

Email updates

Email updates about STC events, Open Decks and opportunities are optional. We only send them when you actively select the separate email consent option. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

WhatsApp community

The WhatsApp invitation is sent to the email address you provide. After joining, WhatsApp processes your account and community activity under its own terms and privacy information. Do not share the invitation link publicly.

Payments & fraud prevention

Stripe only loads on the donation page after you allow this optional category. Stripe may process payment details, device information and activity data to provide the payment service and help detect fraud. STC does not receive your full card details.

Donation records

When a donation succeeds, Stripe sends STC a secure server-to-server notification. STC stores a minimal record containing amount, currency, payment status, Stripe identifiers and timestamps for statistics and duplicate protection. This webhook does not store the donor's name, email address, IP address or card details.

Your choices and rights

You can change your website privacy settings using the button below. You can also ask STC to access, correct or remove your community registration data, or withdraw your email consent.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your registration can be sent through our Instagram account until a dedicated privacy email address is published.
